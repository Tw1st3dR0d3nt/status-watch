Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information StatusWatch collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

Effective date: August 13, 2026

1. Overview

StatusWatch is a Discord application designed to provide real-time incident monitoring and service-status notifications within Discord servers.

We aim to collect and retain only the information reasonably necessary for StatusWatch to provide and maintain its functionality.

2. Information StatusWatch May Process

Depending on how StatusWatch is configured and used, the application may process limited Discord-related information necessary to provide its functionality.

This may include:

  • Discord server identifiers needed to associate configuration with a particular server.
  • Discord channel identifiers used for configured incident notifications.
  • Discord user identifiers when required to identify the administrator performing a configuration action.
  • Configuration information necessary for StatusWatch to operate within a server.
  • Incident and status information generated or retrieved as part of StatusWatch's monitoring functionality.

StatusWatch does not intentionally collect information such as passwords, Discord account credentials, payment card information, or the contents of private messages.

3. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

StatusWatch is not designed to collect or maintain personal profiles about Discord users.

StatusWatch does not intentionally collect or store:

  • Discord account passwords.
  • Authentication credentials other than the bot's own operational credentials.
  • Payment or financial information.
  • Private messages or direct-message conversations.
  • Unrelated message content.

4. How Information Is Used

Information processed by StatusWatch is used only as reasonably necessary to operate, configure, maintain, secure, and improve the application.

Examples include associating a configured notification channel with a Discord server, processing incident information, maintaining configuration state, and troubleshooting operational problems.

5. Server Configuration

StatusWatch supports configuration on a server-by-server basis.

A server's configuration may include information such as the Discord server identifier and the channel designated to receive incident notifications.

Configuration for one Discord server is intended to remain separate from configuration belonging to other servers.

6. Incident and Runtime Data

StatusWatch may maintain operational data necessary to track incidents and provide incident timelines or updates.

Such information is used to support the application's incident-monitoring functionality and is not intended to be used to build advertising profiles or sell personal information.

Operational data may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, troubleshoot issues, maintain continuity, or satisfy applicable operational requirements.

7. Discord API

StatusWatch uses Discord's services and APIs to provide its functionality.

Information available to StatusWatch through Discord is subject to Discord's own policies and technical requirements.

You can review Discord's Privacy Policy for information about how Discord handles information on its platform.

8. Third-Party Services

StatusWatch may rely on third-party services to provide or support monitoring functionality.

When StatusWatch obtains incident or service-status information from an external provider, that provider may independently process information according to its own privacy policy and terms.

StatusWatch does not control the privacy practices of third-party services.

9. Data Sharing

StatusWatch does not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to operate the service, comply with applicable law, protect the security of the service, or respond to legitimate legal requests.

StatusWatch may also rely on service providers or infrastructure providers necessary to operate the application.

10. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information handled by StatusWatch.

These measures may include access controls, restricted configuration access, environment-based credential management, and other appropriate safeguards.

However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Data Retention

StatusWatch retains operational and configuration information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information, operational requirements, and whether the information is necessary to maintain the application's functionality.

12. Removing StatusWatch

A server administrator may remove StatusWatch from a Discord server at any time.

Removing the application may prevent StatusWatch from continuing to access information available through that server. Previously stored operational or configuration information may remain for a reasonable period when necessary for backups, security, troubleshooting, or legitimate operational purposes.

13. Children's Privacy

StatusWatch is intended to operate as a Discord application and does not intentionally collect personal information from children for the purpose of creating individual user profiles.

Users should comply with Discord's age requirements and applicable laws when using Discord and StatusWatch.

14. Your Choices

Server administrators control whether StatusWatch is installed and configured within their Discord server.

If you have a question about information associated with a StatusWatch configuration or believe information has been processed improperly, you may contact the StatusWatch operator using the contact method provided by the StatusWatch project or its associated GitHub repository.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to StatusWatch, its data practices, applicable requirements, or operational procedures.

The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

16. Contact

Questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy should be directed to the StatusWatch operator through the contact method provided by the StatusWatch project or its associated GitHub repository.

Important:

This Privacy Policy is provided as a practical template for the StatusWatch project and is not legal advice. If StatusWatch becomes a commercial service, processes significant amounts of personal information, or is used across multiple jurisdictions, professional legal review is recommended.
