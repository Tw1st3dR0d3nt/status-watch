Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information StatusWatch processes, how it is used, and how it is retained.

Effective date: August 14, 2026

1. Overview

StatusWatch is a Discord application that polls DiscordStatus.com for reported incidents and delivers incident notifications to configured Discord channels.

StatusWatch is designed to process only the information reasonably necessary to operate, configure, and maintain this functionality.

2. Information StatusWatch May Process

StatusWatch may process limited Discord-related information required for its operation. This may include:

  • Discord server (guild) IDs used to associate configuration with a server.
  • Discord channel IDs used for incident notifications.
  • Discord user IDs used for access-control and blacklist functionality.
  • Discord message IDs associated with incident notifications so existing notifications can be updated.
  • Incident IDs and incident-update IDs used to track incidents that have already been processed.
  • Configuration and operational state necessary for StatusWatch to function.

StatusWatch may also receive limited information from Discord while operating, such as server and channel information needed to perform configured actions.

3. Blacklists and Access Control

StatusWatch includes administrative blacklist functionality. Discord user IDs may be stored when a user is blacklisted, and Discord server IDs may be stored when a server is blacklisted.

These identifiers are used only to determine whether StatusWatch should allow its commands or functionality to be used by the affected user or server.

4. Incident and Notification Data

StatusWatch retrieves incident information from DiscordStatus.com, including incident identifiers, incident updates, status information, names, timelines, and incident links.

StatusWatch maintains limited operational state so it can determine whether an incident or update has already been processed and update existing Discord notifications when an incident changes.

Resolved incidents are removed from active incident tracking after the associated notification updates have been successfully processed.

5. How Information Is Used

Information processed by StatusWatch is used only as reasonably necessary to:

  • configure incident notifications for Discord servers;
  • deliver and update incident notifications;
  • enforce administrative blacklist and access controls;
  • maintain incident-tracking state; and
  • troubleshoot and maintain the application.

StatusWatch does not use this information for advertising profiles or targeted advertising.

6. Information Not Intentionally Collected

StatusWatch is not designed to intentionally collect or store:

  • Discord account passwords;
  • payment or financial information;
  • private messages or direct-message conversations;
  • Discord authentication credentials belonging to users; or
  • unrelated message content for storage or profiling.

StatusWatch uses Discord's APIs and permissions as necessary for the bot to operate, but the application does not intentionally retain unrelated Discord content.

7. Discord and Third-Party Services

StatusWatch operates through Discord and uses Discord's services and APIs. Information available to StatusWatch through Discord is also subject to Discord's own policies and technical requirements.

StatusWatch retrieves incident information from DiscordStatus.com. DiscordStatus.com is an independent third-party service and may process information according to its own terms and privacy practices.

StatusWatch does not control the privacy practices of Discord, DiscordStatus.com, or other third-party services.

You can review Discord's Privacy Policy for information about Discord's handling of information on its platform.

8. Data Sharing

StatusWatch does not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to operate the application, comply with applicable law, protect the security of the service, or respond to legitimate legal requests.

9. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by StatusWatch, including restricting access to the bot's operational systems and configuration data.

No electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Retention

StatusWatch retains configuration, blacklist, message, and incident-tracking information only for as long as it is reasonably necessary for the functionality described in this policy.

Retention varies by data type. Incident-tracking state may be removed after an incident is successfully resolved and processed. Configuration and blacklist information may remain until it is changed or removed as part of normal application administration.

11. Removing StatusWatch

A server administrator may remove StatusWatch from a Discord server at any time.

Removing StatusWatch prevents it from continuing to operate in that server. Configuration or other stored information may remain for a reasonable period when necessary for operational, security, troubleshooting, or administrative purposes.

12. Children's Privacy

StatusWatch is a Discord application and does not intentionally create personal profiles for children. Users should comply with Discord's age requirements and applicable laws when using Discord and StatusWatch.

13. Your Choices

Server administrators control whether StatusWatch is installed and configured within their Discord server.

If you have questions about information associated with StatusWatch or believe information has been processed improperly, you may contact the StatusWatch operator through the contact method provided by the StatusWatch project or its associated website.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to StatusWatch, its data practices, applicable requirements, or operational procedures.

The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

15. Contact

Questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy should be directed to the StatusWatch operator through the contact method provided by the StatusWatch project or its associated website.

Important:

This Privacy Policy is provided for the StatusWatch project and is not legal advice. Professional legal review may be appropriate if StatusWatch expands significantly or its data practices change.
